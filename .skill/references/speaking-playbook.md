# Speaking Playbook (P1/P2/P3)

总时长 11-14 分钟,分三部分。考官按 FC/LR/GRA/Pron 四项打分。

---

## Part 1 (4-5 分钟,熟悉话题)

考官从 3 个话题轮换问 (Work/Study, Hometown, Hobbies, Weather 等),每个话题 3-4 题。

### 应答原则

- **2-3 句**: 一句话答完 = 5.5。最少做到 观点 + 理由 + 例子/感受
- **不要背模板**: 用 actually / honestly / well / to be honest 自然化
- **答完即停**: 不要往 P2 长度发展

### 万能延展套路

| 模板 | 例 |
|---|---|
| 观点 + 原因 | "I'd say... because..." |
| 观点 + 对比 | "I prefer X over Y, mainly because..." |
| 观点 + 例子 | "Yes, definitely. For instance, last week I..." |
| 观点 + 频率/程度 | "It really depends. Most days I..., but sometimes..." |
| 观点 + 让步 | "Generally yes, though there are times when..." |

### 高频缓冲短语

- That's a tough one.
- I've never really thought about that, but...
- Honestly speaking...
- Let me think for a second.
- Off the top of my head...

### 常见话题清单 (Q2 2026 见 speaking-topics-2026Q2.md)

Work/Study · Hometown · Accommodation · Hobbies · Weather · Food · Music · Sports · Travel · Friends · Family · Festivals · Reading · Movies · Internet · Mobile phones · Photos · Shopping · Sleep · Weekends

## Part 2 (1 分钟准备 + 1-2 分钟独白)

考官给一张 cue card,4 个 bullet points,1 分钟笔记后说 1.5-2 分钟。

### 笔记策略 (1 分钟)

按 cue card 四点列 **关键词 + 一两个具体细节**,不写完整句:

```
Describe a time you helped someone:
- Who: 邻居老奶奶 Mrs Li
- When: 去年冬天 / last winter
- What: 雪天扫雪 + 买药
- Feel: warm, sense of fulfillment
```

### 答题结构 (2 分钟)

| 时段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 开头 10 秒 | 引入,改写 cue card 第一句 |
| 主体 90 秒 | 按 bullet 顺序,但灵活分配。在最重要那点上多说 |
| 结尾 20 秒 | 总结感受 + 升华 |

### 开头模板

- I'd like to talk about...
- What immediately comes to mind is...
- Today I want to share...
- The example I want to share is from...

### 结尾模板

- ...and that's something I'll never forget.
- ...which is why this experience has stayed with me.
- ...I really treasure this moment / memory.
- ...so this is the X I want to talk about.

### 高分要素

- **时态多样**: 现在 (背景) → 过去 (事件) → 现在完成 (影响) → 将来 (展望)
- **5 感细节**: 具体的视觉/听觉/嗅觉描写比抽象形容词更有说服力
- **情感词**: not just "happy" — exhilarated / relieved / grateful / moved
- **延展技巧**: 任何回答都可以加 "and it reminded me of..." / "looking back now..."

### 跑题怎么办

不要中途纠正。继续讲完。考官只在乎你能不能流利表达 2 分钟,不那么严格扣题。

### 如果没准备好

- 编造合理的故事。考官不在乎真假,只在乎语言。
- 用通用模板套话题: 任何"描述人"都可以套朋友/家人; 任何"描述地方"都可以套你的城市某处。

## Part 3 (4-5 分钟,抽象/社会话题)

P2 话题的延伸,4-6 个开放问题。考官可能追问/打断/反驳。

### 题型识别

| 题型 | 标志 | 应对 |
|---|---|---|
| Compare | What's the difference between X and Y? | 主要差异 + 例子 |
| Opinion | Do you think...? Why? | 表态 + 2 个理由 + 例子 |
| Prediction | How will X change in 10 years? | 趋势预测 + 原因 + 让步 |
| Reason | Why do people...? | 2-3 个原因,分层说 |
| Hypothetical | What would happen if...? | 虚拟语气 + 推演 |
| Solution | How can we solve...? | 多方解决 (政府/个人/社会) |

### 答题套路 (DRER)

- **D**irect: 直接表态 (1 句)
- **R**eason: 原因 (1-2 个)
- **E**xample: 例子 / 数据 / 国家
- **R**efine: 让步 / 反方 / 调和

**例**: 
Q: Do you think the internet has made people less social?
> Direct: I'd say it's a mixed bag, but on balance, yes, to some extent.
> Reason: While we connect with more people online, those connections tend to be shallower.
> Example: Take family dinners — many people now scroll through their phones instead of talking.
> Refine: That said, the internet has also enabled meaningful long-distance friendships, so the picture isn't entirely negative.

### 高分加分项

- **复杂结构**: 虚拟语气 (If governments invested more...) / 倒装 (Not only does X..., but Y also...)
- **抽象词汇**: phenomenon / paradigm / implications / ramifications / underlying causes
- **承认复杂性**: It's not a black-and-white issue. / There are multiple layers to this.
- **数据/研究引用**: Studies have shown... / According to a recent survey...

### 听不懂题怎么办

- Sorry, could you rephrase that?
- Do you mean...? (用自己的话复述确认)
- That's an interesting question. Let me think about it from a different angle...
- I'm not entirely sure what you mean by X, but I'll take a guess...

绝对不要沉默 > 5 秒。

## 整体战术

### 心态
- 把考官当成英语好的朋友, 不是审判官
- 错就错了, 继续说; 不要回头改半天 (打断流畅)

### 节奏
- 正常语速 (130-150 词/分钟),不要快
- 重音清楚, 句末降调清楚

### 流利度伪装
- 用 well / actually / you know 自然填充, 比"嗯..."好
- 一句说完用半秒 pause 而不是 umm

### 不要做的事
- ❌ 背模板 (考官秒识别)
- ❌ 用太多 advanced 词且用错
- ❌ 反复 "I think... I think..."
- ❌ 答完 P1 题 2 分钟 (那是 P2 的活)
