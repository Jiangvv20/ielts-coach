# Speaking Topics · 2026 Q2 (5-8 月题库)

> ⚠️ **题库时效**: 雅思口语题每季度换题 (1-4 / 5-8 / 9-12)。本文件覆盖 **2026 年 5-8 月**。每季度初需手动更新。下次更新提醒: 2026-09-01。
>
> 题目来源: 历年口语题型规律 + 当季常见话题汇编。考试时实际题目可能略有变化,但话题与思路通常一致。

---

## Part 1 (高频话题 + 题目)

每个话题考官问 3-4 题。以下是 5-8 月常见话题,按热度排序。

### 1. Work or Study
- Are you working or studying?
- What do you like / dislike about your job/major?
- Why did you choose this job/major?
- Would you like to change your job/major in the future?

### 2. Hometown
- Where is your hometown?
- What do you like about it?
- Has it changed much in recent years?
- Would you like to live there in the future?

### 3. Accommodation (Flat/House)
- Do you live in a flat or a house?
- What's your favourite room?
- Would you like to change your accommodation?
- What kind of home would you like in the future?

### 4. Hobbies / Free time
- What do you do in your free time?
- Have your hobbies changed since you were a child?
- Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities?
- Is it important to have a hobby?

### 5. Mobile Phones
- When did you get your first mobile phone?
- How often do you use it?
- What apps do you use most?
- Do you think you spend too much time on it?

### 6. Reading
- Do you like reading?
- What kind of books do you read?
- Do you read paper books or e-books?
- Did your parents read to you when you were young?

### 7. Music
- What kind of music do you like?
- Do you play any instruments?
- Has your taste in music changed?
- Do you like listening to live music?

### 8. Food / Cooking
- What's your favourite food?
- Do you cook at home?
- Did you learn cooking from your family?
- Do you prefer eating at home or at restaurants?

### 9. Weather
- What's the weather like in your country?
- What's your favourite season?
- Does the weather affect your mood?
- Do you watch weather forecasts?

### 10. Sleep
- How many hours do you sleep?
- Do you take naps in the afternoon?
- Has your sleep pattern changed?
- What do you do before sleeping?

### 11. Photographs
- Do you like taking photos?
- Do you prefer taking photos of people or scenery?
- Do you keep photos on your phone or print them?
- Have you ever sent photos to others?

### 12. Shopping
- Do you like shopping?
- Do you prefer shopping online or in stores?
- What was the last thing you bought?
- Do you make shopping lists?

### 13. Friends
- Do you have many friends?
- How often do you meet your friends?
- What do you do with friends?
- Is it easier to make friends now than when you were younger?

### 14. Weekends
- What do you usually do on weekends?
- Do you prefer to relax or be active?
- Do weekends feel long enough?
- What did you do last weekend?

### 15. Festivals / Public holidays
- What's the most important festival in your country?
- How do you celebrate it?
- Do you prefer celebrating with family or friends?
- Have festival celebrations changed?

### 16. Email / Messaging
- Do you write emails often?
- Do you prefer email or instant messages?
- What kind of emails do you receive?
- Have you ever received an important email?

### 17. Walking
- Do you like walking?
- Do you walk for exercise or for getting somewhere?
- Where do you usually walk?
- Did you walk more as a child?

### 18. Names
- Does your name have a special meaning?
- Are there naming traditions in your country?
- Would you change your name?
- How do parents in your country choose names?

### 19. Patience
- Are you a patient person?
- When do you find it hard to be patient?
- Have you become more patient as you grew up?
- Is patience important?

### 20. Plants / Flowers
- Do you like flowers?
- Do you grow any plants at home?
- Do you give flowers as gifts?
- What flowers are popular in your country?

## Part 2 (Cue Cards)

每张卡片 4 个 bullet,1 分钟准备 + 1-2 分钟独白。

### 人物类

1. **Describe a person who taught you something useful**
   - Who this person is
   - What they taught you
   - How they taught you
   - Why it was useful

2. **Describe an interesting old person you have met**
   - Who they are
   - How you met them
   - What you talked about
   - Why you found them interesting

3. **Describe a child you know who you think is well-behaved**
   - Who the child is
   - How you know them
   - What they do that is well-behaved
   - How you feel about them

4. **Describe a person you know who has interesting ideas or opinions**
   - Who they are
   - How you know them
   - What kind of ideas they have
   - Why you think their ideas are interesting

5. **Describe a person you would like to study/work with**
   - Who they are
   - What you know about them
   - Why you would like to study/work with them
   - How you can benefit

### 地点类

6. **Describe a place you visited where the air was polluted**
   - Where it was
   - When you went
   - Why the air was polluted
   - How you felt about it

7. **Describe a quiet place you like to go**
   - Where it is
   - How often you go there
   - What you do there
   - How you feel about it

8. **Describe a beautiful city you have visited**
   - Where it is
   - When you went
   - What you did there
   - Why you thought it was beautiful

9. **Describe a café or restaurant you like to go to**
   - Where it is
   - What kind of place it is
   - What you usually have there
   - Why you like it

10. **Describe a historical building you have visited**
    - Where it is
    - What it looks like
    - What you did there
    - What you learned from your visit

### 物品类

11. **Describe a piece of technology you find useful**
    - What it is
    - When you got it
    - How you use it
    - Why it is useful

12. **Describe a gift you gave to someone**
    - What it was
    - Who you gave it to
    - Why you chose it
    - How they reacted

13. **Describe a piece of clothing you often wear**
    - What it is
    - When you got it
    - When you wear it
    - Why you like it

14. **Describe a website you visit often**
    - What the website is
    - How you found it
    - What you do on it
    - Why you visit it often

15. **Describe an item of yours you have had for a long time**
    - What it is
    - How long you have had it
    - Where you got it
    - Why you have kept it

### 事件/经历类

16. **Describe a time you helped someone**
    - Who you helped
    - When and where it happened
    - How you helped them
    - How you felt about it

17. **Describe a time you received good news**
    - What the news was
    - Where you were
    - How you heard about it
    - How you reacted

18. **Describe a time you were stuck in a traffic jam**
    - When it happened
    - Where you were
    - How long you were stuck
    - How you felt

19. **Describe a skill you learned from an older person**
    - What it was
    - Who taught you
    - How you learned it
    - How you feel about this skill

20. **Describe a long walk you enjoyed**
    - Where you walked
    - Who you were with
    - What you saw / did along the way
    - Why you enjoyed it

21. **Describe a time you got up early**
    - When it was
    - Why you got up early
    - What you did
    - How you felt

22. **Describe a course you would like to take**
    - What it is about
    - Where you would take it
    - When you would take it
    - Why it interests you

23. **Describe a time you saw something interesting on social media**
    - What it was
    - Where you saw it
    - What was special about it
    - How you felt about it

24. **Describe a popular product made in your region**
    - What it is
    - What it is made of
    - Who buys it
    - Why it is popular

25. **Describe a rule at your school you agree or disagree with**
    - What the rule is
    - Why the rule exists
    - Whether you agree
    - How students feel about it

## Part 3 (高频拓展话题)

按 P2 话题对应延展。考官追问 4-6 题。

### 教育与学习 (对应 P2 #1, #5, #19, #22, #25)
- Do you think children should be taught by old people?
- What can young people learn from old people?
- Should adults continue learning new things?
- Why do some people not want to learn new skills?
- Is online learning as effective as classroom learning?

### 城市与环境 (对应 P2 #6, #8, #10)
- What are the main causes of air pollution?
- Should governments do more to protect the environment?
- Why do some old buildings need to be preserved?
- How does living in a city affect people's health?
- Do you think future cities will be more environmentally friendly?

### 技术与社会 (对应 P2 #11, #14, #23)
- How has technology changed the way people communicate?
- Are people too dependent on technology?
- What are the disadvantages of social media?
- Should children be allowed to use smartphones?
- Will AI replace human jobs in the future?

### 人际关系 (对应 P2 #1-5, #16)
- Why do some people find it difficult to help others?
- Should we help strangers?
- Do people today help others as much as before?
- What qualities make a good friend?
- How do you maintain friendships when people move away?

### 消费与购物 (对应 P2 #12, #24)
- Why do people enjoy giving gifts?
- Are people influenced by advertising?
- Is it important to save money?
- Why are some products more popular than others?
- Do online reviews influence what people buy?

### 时间与节奏 (对应 P2 #18, #20, #21)
- Why do some people prefer to wake up early?
- Should companies allow flexible working hours?
- How do people deal with stress from traffic?
- Why are people busier now than in the past?
- Is work-life balance achievable?

## 出题原则 (skill 使用本文件时)

- Part 1: 一次抽 1 个话题的 3-4 题(不要混话题)
- Part 2: 一次给 1 张 cue card,显示 4 个 bullets 和准备时间提示
- Part 3: 抽与 P2 对应的话题,4-6 题逐题问
- 抽题尽量覆盖用户的弱项 (从 profile.json 推断)
- 用户回答后,按 §6.4 批改格式输出 (FC/LR/GRA + Pron 建议)
