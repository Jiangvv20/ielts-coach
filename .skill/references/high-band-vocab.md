## 万能高分句型 (适用各话题)

1. **强调**: It is no exaggeration to say that X has fundamentally reshaped...
2. **因果**: A direct consequence of this trend is that...
3. **让步**: While X undoubtedly has its merits, it would be naïve to overlook Y.
4. **倒装**: Not only does X benefit individuals, but it also...
5. **虚拟**: Were governments to invest more heavily in renewable energy, the long-term gains would far outweigh the costs.
6. **强调句**: It is the failure to address root causes that perpetuates the problem.
7. **比较**: The benefits of X far outweigh / pale in comparison to those of Y.
8. **总结**: All things considered, X represents a double-edged sword that requires careful navigation.

## 使用建议 (skill 改写时)

- **不要堆砌**: 一段中 2-3 个 high-band 词即可,过多反不自然
- **搭配优先**: 用 of paramount importance 优于 very important; come up with 优于 think of
- **替换重复**: 同段不重复用同一动词;用代词 / this trend / such practice
- **改写贵在地道**: 把 "I think it is important" 改成 "It is, in my view, of utmost importance",而不是堆 4 个 idioms
