## GRAMMAR (中国学生常错点)

```
grammar | a number of + 复数 + 动词复数 | A number of people are concerned | Not "is concerned". 当主语强调"许多人"动词用复数。
grammar | the number of + 复数 + 动词单数 | The number of users has grown | "the number" 本身是单数。
grammar | depend on (不是 depend of) | It depends on the context | 常错: depend of / depend in。
grammar | be good at + n./V-ing | She is good at painting / at maths | 不能用 to + V。
grammar | look forward to + V-ing | I look forward to meeting you | to 是介词,后接动名词不是不定式。
grammar | 时态: 已发生在过去 → 过去时 | I went to Beijing last year | 不要用现在完成时 with last/yesterday/ago。
grammar | 第三条件 (与过去相反) | If I had known, I would have told you | had + p.p. / would have + p.p.
grammar | 主谓一致: each / every + 单数 | Each student is required to... | 不是 are。
grammar | 主谓一致: neither A nor B → 跟近原则 | Neither he nor his friends are here | 跟最近的 friends。
grammar | 形容词比较级修饰 much/far/a lot | This is much better than | 不是 very better。
grammar | 不可数名词 | information / advice / knowledge / equipment | 都不能加 s 也不能用 a/an。
grammar | 介词 in/on/at 时间 | in 2025 / on Monday / at 3pm | in 大段, on 一天, at 时刻。
grammar | between A and B / among 3+ | between you and me / among the students | 二者间用 between。
grammar | as if / as though + 虚拟 | He acts as if he were the boss | were 不是 was。
grammar | 关系代词: that vs which | The car that broke down / The car, which broke down, ... | that 限定;which 非限定。
```

# 伪代码

  TYPE, FRONT, BACK, CONTEXT = line.split(' | ')
  card = {

    "type": TYPE,

    "back": BACK,
    "context": CONTEXT,
