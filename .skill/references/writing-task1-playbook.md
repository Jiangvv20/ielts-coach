# Writing Task 1 Playbook

20 分钟, ≥150 词。学术类:图表 (Line/Bar/Pie/Table/Process/Map/Mixed)。培训类:Letter (formal/semi-formal/informal)。

---

## 学术类 · 通用结构

```
Para 1: Introduction (改写题目, 1 句)
Para 2: Overview (2-3 句, 总览 2-3 个最显著特征)
Para 3-4: Body (具体数据描述, 分组对比)
```

**Overview 是 7+ 必备**。位置可在 Para 2(推荐)或最后。

---

## 题型 1: Line Chart (线图)

**关键**: 时间维度 → 描述趋势 + 转折点 + 起止对比。

**Trend 动词**:
- 上升: rise / increase / grow / climb / surge / soar / rocket
- 下降: fall / decrease / decline / drop / plunge / plummet / slump
- 平稳: remain stable / level off / plateau / hold steady
- 波动: fluctuate / oscillate / vary

**程度副词**: dramatically / sharply / significantly / steadily / gradually / slightly / marginally

**例**: The number of visitors **rose dramatically from 100,000 in 2000 to a peak of 500,000 in 2010, before falling sharply to 200,000 by 2020**.

---

## 题型 2: Bar Chart (柱图)

**关键**: 对比类别 → 最大/最小/差距/分组。

**对比短语**:
- A was **roughly twice as high as** B
- A **outnumbered** B by a factor of three
- A was **comparable to** B
- Whereas A stood at 30%, B accounted for only 10%
- The figure for A was substantially higher than that of B

---

## 题型 3: Pie Chart (饼图)

**关键**: 比例 + 排序 + 加总检查。

**比例词**:
- 大: the majority / the lion's share / accounted for ... / made up ...
- 小: a small fraction / a tiny proportion / merely ...
- 中: roughly a quarter / about one-third / approximately half

**例**: Housing constituted the largest share at 40%, followed by food at 25%, while transport and entertainment made up the remaining third combined.

---

## 题型 4: Table (表格)

**关键**: 数据多 → 必须分组,不要逐格描述。

**分组策略**:
- 按列(类别)分组 vs 按行(时间/国家)分组,选信息更密的那个
- 找最高/最低 + 最大变化 + 异常值

---

## 题型 5: Process (流程图)

**关键**: 步骤词 + 被动语态 + 时间顺序。

**步骤词**: initially / firstly / subsequently / following this / once X is done / at this stage / finally

**被动语态(常用)**: The raw materials are first collected, after which they are transported to the factory, where they are processed.

**循环 vs 线性**: cyclical process (returns to the start) / linear process (ends at a fixed product)

---

## 题型 6: Map (地图变化)

**关键**: 时态 + 方位 + 变化动词。

**时态**: 过去 → 现在/未来 (was vs is) 或 现在 → 未来 (is vs will be)

**方位**: to the north of / adjacent to / opposite to / in the centre of / along the eastern edge

**变化动词**: 
- 新建: be constructed / be built / emerge / appear
- 拆除: be demolished / be removed / be replaced by
- 改建: be converted into / be transformed into / be expanded
- 不变: remain unchanged / stay the same

---

## 题型 7: Mixed (混合图)

**关键**: 两个图必须**互相对照**找出关系,不要写成两段独立描述。

---

## Task 1 高分句型 (Body 段)

- It is clear from the chart that...
- Of particular note is the fact that...
- A striking feature is...
- The most pronounced change occurred between X and Y, when...
- This was in stark contrast to...

## 培训类 · Letter

### 三种风格识别

| 类型 | 收信人 | 称呼 | 结尾 | 用语 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formal | 不认识 (manager/官方) | Dear Sir/Madam | Yours faithfully | 完整句, 不用缩略 |
| Semi-formal | 认识但有距离 (老板/导师) | Dear Mr/Ms Smith | Yours sincerely | 适度正式 |
| Informal | 朋友家人 | Dear John / Hi Sarah | Best wishes / Take care | 缩略可, 短句 |

### 结构

```
开头: Reason for writing (1 句)
主体: 3 个 bullet 各展开 1-2 句
结尾: Action wanted / Looking forward
落款
```

### 高频题型

1. **投诉信** (formal): 描述问题 + 影响 + 期望解决
2. **申请信** (formal): 身份 + 资格 + 联系方式
3. **邀请信** (informal/semi): 场合 + 时间地点 + 期待回复
4. **道歉信** (informal/semi): 道歉 + 原因 + 弥补
5. **建议信** (informal/semi): 引入 + 三条建议 + 鼓励选择

## 出题示例 (skill 生成时参考此格式)

### 例 1 (Line Chart)
The graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries (Country A, B, C) between 2000 and 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

### 例 2 (Process)
The diagram below illustrates the process of manufacturing chocolate from cocoa beans.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features.

### 例 3 (Letter - formal)
You recently bought a piece of furniture from a large store but were disappointed with its quality. Write a letter to the store manager. In your letter:
- describe the item you bought
- explain what was wrong with it
- state what action you would like them to take

## 出题原则

- 模拟时优先出**用户弱项题型** (从 profile.json 的 weakAreas 推断)
- 数据要合理(不要写 200%)
- 流程图/地图要描述清楚才能写
- 给出题后告知"建议 20 分钟内完成,字数 ≥150"
