# Writing Task 2 Playbook

40 分钟, ≥250 词。议论文,占写作总分 2/3。

---

## 五大题型识别 (按题目结尾判断)

| 题型 | 标志词 | 结构 |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Opinion (同意反对) | To what extent do you agree or disagree? / Do you agree? | 表态 + 单边论证(可让步)|
| 2. Discussion (双边讨论) | Discuss both views and give your own opinion. | 双方观点 + 表态 |
| 3. Advantage/Disadvantage | Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? / What are the pros and cons? | 双方利弊 + (如问 outweigh)表态 |
| 4. Problem/Solution (Causes/Solutions) | What are the causes and what can be done? / What problems does this cause and how to solve? | 原因/问题 + 解决方案 |
| 5. Two-part Question | 包含两个问号 (Why...? What can be done?) | 分别回答两问 |

---

## 通用四段结构 (推荐 7+ 写法)

```
Para 1 (Intro, 50-60 词):
  - Hook (1 句,改写题目背景)
  - Thesis (1-2 句,明确表态 + 预告主体)

Para 2 (Body 1, 70-90 词):
  - Topic sentence (论点 1)
  - Explanation (展开)
  - Example (具体例子)
  - Mini-conclusion / link

Para 3 (Body 2, 70-90 词):
  - 同上结构,论点 2

Para 4 (Conclusion, 40-50 词):
  - 重申观点 (改换说法)
  - 升华 (展望/警示/建议)
```

**字数控制**: 总 270-300 词最佳。低于 250 直接扣 TR。

---

## 各题型详细套路

### 1. Opinion (同意反对)

**两种立场**:
- A. 完全同意/反对 (single-side): 两个 body 都论证你的立场,不写反方
- B. 部分同意 (balanced): 一个 body 同意,一个 body 反对,Conclusion 整合

**例题**: Some people believe that university education should be free for all citizens. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**推荐 A 立场 (Strongly agree)**:
- Body 1: 教育是基本权利 → 促进社会公平 → 例: 北欧国家
- Body 2: 经济收益 → 培养人才 → 例: 高技能劳动力推动 GDP

---

### 2. Discussion (双边讨论)

**结构**:
- Body 1: 第一种观点 (描述 + 客观分析 + 例子)
- Body 2: 第二种观点 (同上)
- Conclusion: 表态 + 简要理由

**关键**: Body 中可以用 "Those who advocate X argue that..." 不直接说"我",到 Conclusion 才表态。

**例题**: Some think children should be educated in single-sex schools, while others believe mixed schools are better. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

---

### 3. Advantage/Disadvantage

**两种问法**:
- A. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? → 必须表态哪边大
- B. What are the advantages and disadvantages? → 只需列举,不必表态

**结构**:
- Body 1: 优点 (1-2 个,展开)
- Body 2: 缺点 (1-2 个,展开)
- Conclusion: (A 问法)表态 + 综合权衡

**注意**: 优缺点要平衡数量,不要 3 个优点 1 个缺点 (会显得偏)。

---

### 4. Problem/Solution

**结构 A (causes + solutions)**:
- Body 1: 原因 (1-2 个根本原因)
- Body 2: 解决方案 (对应原因的措施)
- Conclusion: 强调多方努力

**结构 B (problems + solutions)**:
- Body 1: 问题 (1-2 个具体问题)
- Body 2: 解决方案

**例题**: Air pollution in big cities is getting worse. What causes this problem and what measures could governments and individuals take?

---

### 5. Two-part Question

**结构**: 每个 Body 段对应一个子问题,简单粗暴。

**例题**: Why are more people choosing to live alone? Is this a positive or negative trend?
- Body 1: 原因 (经济独立 / 个人主义文化)
- Body 2: 评价 (我认为更多是 negative,因为...)

---

## 论证套路 (PEEL+)

每个 Body 段内部:

- **P**oint: 主题句,直接抛论点
- **E**xplanation: 展开原因,3-4 句
- **E**xample: 具体例子 (国家/数据/历史/个人观察)
- **L**ink: 链接到下一段或重申论点

**升级版 (PEEL + Counter)**: 加入让步 + 反驳
- ... Admittedly, critics may argue that X. However, this overlooks the fact that...

---

## 高分句型清单

### 引入观点
- It is widely believed that...
- A common perception is that...
- There has been considerable debate over...
- In contemporary society, the issue of X has gained prominence.

### 表态
- I firmly believe / am convinced that...
- I largely agree with this view, although with some reservations.
- While X holds some merit, I find Y far more compelling.

### 因果
- This stems from / is attributable to / can be traced back to...
- ..., which in turn leads to / results in / gives rise to...
- A direct consequence of this is...

### 让步反驳
- Admittedly, X has its merits; however,...
- While it is true that..., the reality is that...
- Critics may contend that..., yet this argument fails to consider...

### 举例
- Take X as a case in point.
- A striking example can be found in...
- This is exemplified by...
- Recent studies / research has shown that...

### 结尾
- To sum up, while both views have validity, the evidence suggests that...
- In conclusion, X is a complex issue that requires a balanced approach.
- Looking ahead, it is imperative that... if we are to...

## 八大高频话题 + 思路

| 话题 | 常见角度 |
|---|---|
| Education | 应试 vs 素质 / 公私立 / 网络教育 / 学科价值 |
| Environment | 污染原因 / 个人 vs 政府责任 / 可再生能源 |
| Technology | 利弊 (沟通/工作/隐私) / AI 替代工作 / 屏幕时间 |
| Government | 公共服务投资 / 法律严厉度 / 国际援助 |
| Family | 大家庭 vs 核家庭 / 父母角色 / 代沟 |
| Work | WFH / 工作生活平衡 / 跳槽 / 自动化 |
| Health | 公共医疗 / 饮食习惯 / 心理健康 |
| Globalization | 文化同质化 / 经济一体化 / 语言消亡 |

每个话题准备 2-3 个万能例子 (国家/数据/研究),写作时套用。

## 写作时间分配 (40 分钟)

- 5 分钟: 审题 + 列大纲 (论点 + 例子 + 让步)
- 30 分钟: 写作
- 5 分钟: 检查 (拼写 / 时态 / 单复数 / 冠词)

---

## 出题示例 (skill 生成参考)

### 例 1 (Opinion)
Some people believe that governments should invest more in public transportation rather than building new roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 例 2 (Discussion)
Some people think that schools should focus on academic subjects, while others believe that practical skills are more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### 例 3 (Pros/Cons)
In many countries, people are choosing to work from home instead of in an office. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

### 例 4 (Problem/Solution)
In recent years, many shops and restaurants in city centers have been replaced by online services. What problems does this cause and what can be done to address them?

### 例 5 (Two-part)
More and more young people are using social media to communicate. Why is this happening, and is it a positive or negative development?
