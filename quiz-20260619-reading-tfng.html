Passage 节选 — Criminal Justice Reform: Beyond the Prison Cell

For decades, most Western nations have relied on incarceration as the cornerstone of criminal justice. Yet a growing body of research challenges the assumption that longer sentences are the most effective deterrent to crime. A landmark report by the National Academies of Sciences concluded that the certainty of punishment — not its severity — is the stronger deterrent. In other words, offenders are more effectively discouraged by the likelihood of being caught than by the length of any subsequent sentence.

The fiscal consequences of mass incarceration are staggering. In the United States, housing one prisoner costs taxpayers between $30,000 and $60,000 annually, depending on the state. The United Kingdom reports similar figures. These costs have prompted some legislatures to reconsider mandatory minimum sentencing laws and to explore community-based alternatives. Several US states have introduced diversion programmes that redirect non-violent offenders away from custodial sentences toward rehabilitation schemes.

Proponents of restorative justice — an approach that prioritises healing harm over punishing offenders — point to Scandinavia as evidence. Norway, where prison conditions emphasise rehabilitation and skills training, records a recidivism rate of approximately 20%, compared with 40–60% in many other developed nations. Critics, however, caution that direct comparisons are difficult given differences in social welfare systems, income inequality, and drug policy. Some sceptics further argue that restorative justice places an unfair emotional burden on victims, who may feel pressured to forgive.

Penal reform advocates acknowledge these tensions but maintain that purely punitive systems have demonstrably failed. They argue that addressing root causes — poverty, lack of education, substance dependency — is not merely compassionate but fiscally rational. Whether governments will muster the political will to enact such reforms, however, remains deeply uncertain.

Q01 · T/F/NG
The National Academies of Sciences found that the length of a prison sentence is the most effective deterrent to crime.
正确答案: B (False)
原文说报告结论是"the certainty of punishment — not its severity — is the stronger deterrent"，即确定性而非严厉程度更有效，与题目"length … most effective"矛盾。
Q02 · T/F/NG
Norway's recidivism rate is approximately 20%.
正确答案: A (True)
原文明确写道 "Norway … records a recidivism rate of approximately 20%"。
Q03 · T/F/NG
The annual cost of incarcerating one person exceeds $65,000 in all Western countries.
正确答案: B (False)
原文给出的美国数字是 $30,000–$60,000，上限为 $60,000，并未超过 $65,000；且并非"all Western countries"。
Q04 · T/F/NG
Some US states have introduced programmes that divert non-violent offenders away from prison.
正确答案: A (True)
原文：「Several US states have introduced diversion programmes that redirect non-violent offenders away from custodial sentences」，与题目直接一致。
Q05 · T/F/NG
Sceptics of restorative justice argue that it may place an unfair burden on victims.
正确答案: A (True)
原文：「Some sceptics further argue that restorative justice places an unfair emotional burden on victims」，与题目吻合。
Q06 · T/F/NG
All countries that have adopted restorative justice have recorded lower reoffending rates.
正确答案: C (Not Given)
原文只提到挪威的例子，并未列出"所有采用修复性司法的国家"的再犯率数据，无法判断。
Q07 · T/F/NG
The financial cost of incarceration has prompted some governments to reconsider sentencing policy.
正确答案: A (True)
原文：「These costs have prompted some legislatures to reconsider mandatory minimum sentencing laws」，直接支持题目。
Q08 · 词汇填空
The judge imposed a _____ sentence, meaning the offender would serve time in prison. (adj. 监禁的)
正确答案: custodial
a custodial sentence = 监禁判决，与 non-custodial sentence（非监禁判决）相对。见原文 "custodial sentences"。
Q09 · 词汇填空
_____ programmes aim to reform offenders rather than simply punish them. (n. 矫正/康复)
正确答案: rehabilitation
原文提到「rehabilitation schemes」和挪威监狱重视「rehabilitation and skills training」。IELTS 写作高频词。
Q10 · 词汇填空
_____ justice prioritises healing harm over punishing offenders. (adj. 修复性的)
正确答案: restorative
restorative justice = 修复性司法，与 punitive（惩罚性）和 retributive（报复性）相对。原文核心概念。
