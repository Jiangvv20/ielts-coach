Receptionist (Maria) Northfield Community Learning Centre, good morning. How can I help?

Alex Oh, hello. I'd like to register for one of your evening courses, please.

Maria Of course! Could I start with your full name?

Alex Yes, it's Alex Harrington. That's H-A-R-R-I-N-G-T-O-N.

Maria Thank you, Mr Harrington. And a contact number?

Alex My mobile is 07854 331 209.

Maria Perfect. Now, which course were you interested in?

Alex I saw your Advanced Photography course advertised online. Is that still available?

Maria It is, yes. It starts on the 12th of June and runs for eight weeks, on Tuesday evenings, from half past six to half past eight.

Alex That sounds perfect. What's the course fee?

Maria The standard fee is £85. However, if you pay before the 1st of June, there's an early-bird discount — you'd only pay £65.

Alex Oh brilliant. I'll definitely take advantage of that. Do I need to bring any special equipment to the first session?

Maria For the first session, you just need to bring your camera and a notebook. We provide all other materials.

Alex And where exactly is the centre located?

Maria We're on Elmwood Road — but parking is available on Cedar Lane, just around the corner. It's free.

Alex Oh good. And is there a waiting list? I'm a bit worried about whether there are places left.

Maria You're in luck — there are still four places available. I'd recommend registering today to be safe.

Alex Wonderful. I'd like to go ahead and register, then, please.

Maria Excellent. I'll just take a few more details …

✏️ 作答
Q01 · 拼写
What is the applicant's surname? (拼写正确)
注意: 首字母大写也接受
Harrington
定位: "It's Alex Harrington. That's H-A-R-R-I-N-G-T-O-N." → 考官当场拼读。
Q02 · 数字
What is the applicant's mobile number?
原文照抄: 07854 331 209
07854 331 209
定位: "My mobile is 07854 331 209." → 注意空格位置.
Q03 · 词汇
Complete the course name: __________ Photography
Advanced
定位: "I saw your Advanced Photography course advertised online." → 陷阱: 考生可能只写 Photography.
Q04 · 日期
When does the course start? (日期)
也接受: 12 June / June 12 / 12th of June
12th June (也接受 12 June / June 12)
定位: "It starts on the 12th of June" → 英式表达，日在前月在后.
Q05 · 星期
On which day of the week are the classes held?
Tuesday
定位: "on Tuesday evenings" → 注意与 Thursday 的区分 (T-U vs T-H).
Q06 · 价格
What is the standard course fee? (数字即可, 不含 £)
85
定位: "The standard fee is £85." → 表格中 £ 已给出，只填数字.
Q07 · 价格 (早鸟优惠)
What is the early-bird fee if paid before 1 June? (数字即可)
65
定位: "you'd only pay £65" → 陷阱: 先说 £85 再给折扣价。选最终价格.
Q08 · 物品
Besides the camera, what else must the applicant bring to the first session?
notebook
定位: "you just need to bring your camera and a notebook" → 相机已在表格中给出；答案是 notebook.
Q09 · 地点
Where is the free car park located?
Cedar Lane
定位: "parking is available on Cedar Lane" → 地名需精确拼写. 大小写不影响得分.
Q10 · 数字 (文字)
How many places are still available on the course?
也接受: 4
four (也接受 4)
定位: "there are still four places available" → 口语中用单词表达数字，写 four 或 4 均可.

Section 1 核心技巧复盘
① 提前看题: 先扫表格，预判每格答案类型 (人名/数字/地名/物品)
② 听改后: 对话中先说一个选项再更正 → 选最终答案 (Q07 先报 £85 再说 £65)
③ 拼写即得分: 人名地名听到拼读信号 (H-A-R-R…) 立刻逐字写
④ 容错提示: 字母大小写通常不扣分，但单词拼写错误 = 零分
